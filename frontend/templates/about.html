How StudyTrack AI leverages artificial intelligence to revolutionize academic outcomes

🎯

Predictive Risk Modeling

High-accuracy Random Forest classifier continuously evaluates multi-dimensional academic metrics, attendance, stress, and lifestyle habits to compute real-time dropout risk and priority scores.

Random Forest Risk Scoring Priority Engine
🧠

Cluster Personalization

Unsupervised K-Means clustering groups learners into behavioral archetypes. Generates custom study timetables, ideal target adjustments, and tailored study techniques (Pomodoro, Feynman, Spaced Repetition).

K-Means Archetypes Smart Timetables

Habit & Growth Tracking

Interactive student portal features daily productivity logging, timeline journals, and visual growth charts to empower students with continuous feedback and sustainable behavioral transformation.
